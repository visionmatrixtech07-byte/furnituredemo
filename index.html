Good to know

Questions before you buy?

Here are the basics. For exact availability, sizes and pricing, call or message us.

Do you sell to homes and businesses?

Yes. Kolar Home is positioned for retail home purchases as well as office, rental, institutional and bulk requirements.

Can you help me select the right size?

Share your room dimensions or a quick photo on WhatsApp. We can help you shortlist sizes and practical layouts before you visit or order.

Do you provide installation?

Installation support can be checked for the product and delivery location. Ask while requesting your quote.

Are rentals and AMC available for every product?

Availability depends on the furniture type, quantity and requirement. Contact us with the details and we will confirm what is possible.

How do I get a price?

Call 9966196013 or use the WhatsApp buttons. Mention the product, quantity, delivery location and any preferred size or finish.
