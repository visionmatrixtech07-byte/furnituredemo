Do you handle wholesale medicine enquiries?

Yes. Kolar Pharma is positioned for wholesale generic and private or branded medicine conversations for pharmacies, clinics, nursing homes, medical stores and other professional buyers.

Can I ask for generic medicines?

Yes. Share the molecule or medicine name, strength, dosage form, pack size and quantity. Availability is confirmed enquiry by enquiry.

Can I ask for private or branded medicines?

Yes. Share the preferred brand or your private-medicine requirement and ask the team to confirm the available supply route.

What should I send with my enquiry?

Send your business type, Kolar location, medicine name or molecule, strength, pack size and required quantity. A photo of an existing pack can also help clarify the requirement.

Do you provide medical advice or prescriptions?

No. This website is for medicine supply enquiries. Please consult a qualified medical professional for diagnosis, treatment or prescription decisions.
